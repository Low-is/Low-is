## Hi, I'm Lois (Low-is) 👋🏾

I'm a **Data Scientist at Johns Hopkins**, working at the intersection of **pediatric and neonatal research, biostatistics, and data science**.

🔬 My work focuses on using clinical data to better understand heterogeneity in pediatric and neonatal populations. A lot of that involves **phenotyping and characterizing clinically meaningful subgroups**, developing **prediction models**, and using **longitudinal data to model trajectories and forecast outcomes**.

💻 On the technical side, I enjoy building reproducible analytical workflows and working across the data science pipeline—from data wrangling and statistical modeling to machine learning, visualization, and translating results into clinically interpretable findings.

### 🧠 Research interests
- Pediatric & neonatal data science
- Clinical phenotyping & subgroup characterization
- Predictive modeling & machine learning
- Longitudinal data analysis & forecasting
- Biostatistics & clinical research methodology
- Reproducible research & analytical workflows
